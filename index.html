How to play
  • Each round you're dealt 6 cards. Pick 2 for the crib, which belongs to the dealer.
  • A starter card is turned up. A jack scores the dealer 2 (his heels).
  • Pegging: take turns playing cards, keeping the count at 31 or under. Score for 15 (2), 31 (2), pairs (2, 6, 12) and runs of 3 or more.
  • If you can't play without going over 31, say Go. The last player to lay a card scores 1.
  • The show: each hand scores with the starter. Fifteens 2, pairs 2, runs 1 per card, flush 4 or 5, and 1 for a jack matching the starter's suit (his nobs). Then the dealer scores the crib.
  • After each round you both walk your points along the lane. First home at 121 wins.
